# DeltaBot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how DeltaBot processes information when you use the bot.

## 1. Information DeltaBot May Process

Depending on the features you use, DeltaBot may process or store:

- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server IDs.
- Discord channel IDs.
- Discord role IDs.
- Commands and command inputs.
- Bot settings and server configuration.
- Saved favorites, alerts, watchlists, or preferences.
- Usage records, error logs, and security logs.
- Public or third-party game and market data requested through the bot.

DeltaBot does not need your Discord password or authentication token. Do not provide them to the bot.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide bot commands and features.
- Save user or server preferences.
- Deliver alerts or requested information.
- Prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
- Diagnose errors and maintain reliability.
- Improve the bot's performance and functionality.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Data Sharing

DeltaBot does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only:

- With hosting, database, monitoring, or infrastructure providers needed to operate the bot.
- When required by law or a valid legal process.
- To investigate abuse, fraud, security incidents, or violations.
- With your permission.

## 4. Data Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate DeltaBot, provide requested features, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal requirements.

Logs may be deleted or overwritten periodically.

Saved settings, favorites, alerts, or account-related records may remain until they are deleted, the bot is removed, or a deletion request is completed.

## 5. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of information associated with your Discord user ID or server.

To make a request, contact:

xtect on Discord

You may be asked to verify that you control the relevant Discord account or server before data is deleted.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

DeltaBot is not intended for anyone who is not permitted to use Discord under Discord's age requirements or applicable law.

DeltaBot does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 8. Third-Party Services

DeltaBot may interact with Discord, game APIs, market APIs, hosting providers, databases, or other third-party services.

Those services have their own privacy policies and may process information independently.

## 9. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, depending on where the bot's infrastructure and service providers operate.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when DeltaBot's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was most recently revised.
